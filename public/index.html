How it works
1

Workspace admin approves scopes

Your workspace administrator reviews and approves the permissions recall-bot needs to read threads and send you setup instructions.

2

You get a one-time Slack DM

After approval, recall-bot sends you a direct message containing a link to set up your personal dashboard and connect your coding agent.

3

Tag @recall-bot on threads

Capture important threads by tagging the bot. Run /recall-key to get a delegate key that lets your coding agent connect via MCP.
